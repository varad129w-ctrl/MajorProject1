Who we are & Why..

We built the travel planner
So that you need not to.

No endless tabs. No guesswork on budget. Just search a place, pick what you can spend, and get a plan that's ready to follow.

TripCraft is a free travel planning platform built for Indian destinations -- designed to give every traveller, regardless of budget, a clear, honest, actionable trip plan in seconds.

How TripCraft works

Search a destination

Type any city, hill station, beach or heritage town in India. Our search finds it instantly and shows you what's there.

Get your plan

Choose Low, Medium or High budget. TripCraft generates a day-wise itinerary with costs for stay, food, travel and activities.

Travel with clarity

Save your plan, revisit it anytime, and head out knowing exactly where you're going and what it will cost.

Why we built this

Planning a trip in India usually means juggling ten different websites — one for hotels, one for trains, one for "things to do", another for "is it worth it", and eventually a chaotic spreadsheet that still doesn't tell you your total budget.

We wanted one page that gives you everything: the places worth visiting, an honest order to visit them in, and three budget options that reflect what people actually spend — not what travel blogs pretend trips cost.

TripCraft started as a side project by a group of developers who were tired of planning trips the hard way. It's still growing, still free, and built entirely around making Indian travel easier for Indian travellers.
